# Faculty structure and the student's live day

Implementation plan, 12 November 2026. Local implementation and verification are complete; see [decisions and evidence](ACADEMIC_STRUCTURE_AND_DAY.md). Native rollout is tracked in SERVER_READINESS.md.

1. Normalize faculties, programmes and timetable groups, retaining legacy fields/data. A registration programme token maps through the catalogue to its faculty. Academic-year placement is suggested from intake year and then explicitly confirmed; repeat/leave/transfer overrides are retained. Keep sponsorship and registration sequence separate from class identity. Registration format is supported by MUST's published `2024/BCS/000/PS` example; Mark's institutional email convention and current-year examples were supplied by the operator. Derived mailboxes are not verified ownership.
2. Reuse source-backed faculty/programme/year/track ideas from mylectures-2. Match shared groups and exact track aliases, deduplicate equivalent shared events, retain conflicting venues/times rather than silently merging them, and keep unknown groups unassigned. Never copy the app's account-free self-selected programme into SAMS authorization. Attendance still requires explicit enrollment.
3. Lead the student workspace with happening-now/next-up cards, countdown/progress, today's chronological classes and automatic check-in targeting. If multiple actual classes overlap, display the conflict and require choosing one of those candidates. Upcoming or weekly-reference rows never authorize check-in. With no actual enrolled class, show the personal weekly reference clearly labelled. Distinguish a time-based ongoing class from an open lecturer-controlled check-in window. Preserve offline attempts, identity binding and server-time enforcement.
4. Add dependent faculty/programme/group/year/semester/search filters to registrar courses, student setup/enrollment and timetable setup. Filter before returning large lists and bound result sizes. Do not expose the student directory to students. Preserve cross-programme shared-course audiences explicitly; filters are navigation, not a replacement for role/ownership checks.
5. Build a private, repeatable sample import for BSE/BCS/BIT/BBA/BME, preserving the supplied BSE roster and generating ten synthetic identities per other programme. Keep the actual roster/passwords outside Git. Use intake 2025/current Year 2 in 2026/2027 as the initial confirmed placement. Detect duplicate registrations/emails and never overwrite existing identity or enrollment. Do not send email or pretend generated institutional addresses are verified mailboxes.
6. Validate the hierarchy, registration/email/year suggestions, faculty scoping, shared tracks/deduplication, time boundaries, conflicts, automatic targeting and offline preservation. Extend the Postman deliverable and documentation. Take recovery copies before additive live migrations, push detailed commits and deploy the verified release through the native service path.

Other data: source lecturer names are reference, not accounts or teaching permissions. Map to confirmed staff accounts. Create dated classes only from reviewed times/calendar; the operator confirmed the three Thursday BSE II intervals (08–10, 10–13, 14–17), not all institutional exceptions. Course offerings remain separate by academic year/semester; enrollments and effective dates explicitly grant attendance access. No missing attendance is invented from weekly rows. Sample records stay distinguishable from user-supplied records; fees, sponsorship administration and automated academic promotion remain outside attendance scope.
